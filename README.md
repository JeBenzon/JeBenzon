## Eggox

<a href="https://eggox.net"><img src="https://eggvelop.com/img/shots/eggox-crowd.webp" alt="Eggox: players in the town square by the quay" width="100%"></a>

**[Eggox](https://eggox.net)** · browser game, in beta, 18+

A game where everything is built by the players. Start in a space of your own, make anything out of little cubes in the Studio, claim a plot in one long shared world, and meet the people who built the rest, right in the browser. Elixir on the server, Rust compiled to WebAssembly in the browser. I write about building it in [the journal](https://eggvelop.com/blog/eggox-autumn-2026).
